# Content and Image Policy

## Priority

1. Real restaurant photo.
2. Faithful enhancement of a real photo.
3. Short reshoot.
4. Clearly licensed commercial-use stock, temporarily.
5. Owner-approved temporary AI image.
6. No image.

Never copy Google image search results or competitor photos. Never invent portions, ingredients, sourcing, certifications, reviews, discounts, or availability.

Allowed enhancement: exposure, white balance, crop, background cleanup, sharpness, plate-edge cleanup.

Forbidden enhancement: larger portion, different fish, added ingredients, different cooking method, or materially different presentation.
